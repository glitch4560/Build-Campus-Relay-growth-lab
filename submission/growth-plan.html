01 / A specific student, a specific reason to join

Target: final-year BE/BTech students in 10 accessible engineering colleges, starting with CSE/IT/ECE and beginner AI learners. Focus on students who want a demonstrable project for interviews and have a laptop and internet. Other branches are welcome if the workshop suits their skill level. Recruit two class reps, coding-club leads or placement volunteers per college.

Why they care

“I have watched tutorials, but I still do not have a small AI project I can explain.” Placement pressure, limited time and uncertainty about where to start are hypotheses to test in five short student conversations on day 1.

What earns registration

A 20-second project preview, free access, beginner prerequisites, a confirmed time, and a recommendation from a known classmate. Ask only name, email, college and final-year eligibility. Optional reminders; referrals never required.

Known campus senders provide context and access within a week. A concrete build reduces ambiguity; a short form reduces friction. The strongest dependency is access: contact 30 potential champions to activate 20, and approach 8 partners to confirm 4. Neither acceptance rate is guaranteed.

06 / Watch the assumptions

Base: 360 + 80 + 60 = 500.

Lower conversion: campus click 25% and registration 30% gives 225 + 80 + 60 = 365. At 11.25 registrations per new 150-person group, 12 extra groups would be needed. The ₹500 reserve supports only 10 honoraria: improve conversion or add 2 volunteer groups; do not promise the target.

10% overlap: conservative haircut gives 450. The tracker counts duplicate emails once; pre-registration overlap still reduces reach.

A/B pilot is directional, not statistically conclusive. Log delivered posts and unique tracked visits, then form completion. High reach + low clicks: revise hook. Good clicks + low completion: fix clarity or form.

07 / Built: Campus Relay, a working acquisition prototype

Source-coded champion links; a sample registration flow; unique-email checks with first-touch attribution; student referral links; a day-by-day dashboard; forecast sensitivity controls; and CSV export. Seeded with 160 clearly labelled fictional records. It supports the campaign's hardest operational question: which source needs attention today?

Browser-local simulation: no shared database, verified identities, click analytics or message delivery. Production would add those plus organizer authentication. The demo is not the registration system for a real workshop.
